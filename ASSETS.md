# Asset and licence ledger

Every file that ships with Hard Landing is listed in
[`godot/data/asset_ledger.json`](godot/data/asset_ledger.json), along with where it came from and
the licence it is under. `tests/audio_test` fails if a sound in `godot/audio` has no row, if its
source is unknown, or if it falls outside its tier's loudness range.

## Where things come from today

| What | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sound effects (`godot/audio/*.wav`) | Synthesised by `tools/audio/make_sfx.py` | Project original |
| Music and ambience beds (`godot/audio/*.ogg`) | Synthesised by `tools/audio/make_music.py` | Project original |
| Models (`godot/assets/**/*.glb`) | Generated by the bpy scripts in `blender/` | Project original |
| UI fonts (`godot/fonts/`): Barlow, Barlow Condensed | [The Barlow Project Authors](https://github.com/jpt/barlow), from google/fonts | SIL OFL 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt` ships with them) |
| Engine | Godot Engine 4.7 | MIT |

The only third-party files are the fonts. The OFL lets them ship inside a commercial game, as long as the licence goes with them and the fonts are not sold on their own.

## Adding a third-party file

1. Check that its licence allows commercial use in a Godot game and redistribution inside a
   packed build. CC0 is fine. CC-BY is fine with credit. Do not use "non-commercial" or
   "editorial only" licences.
   - Quixel Megascans from Fab: check each listing's licence and confirm it allows use outside
     Unreal before adding it. Record the Fab listing URL, the licence name and the download
     date.
2. Add a source to `sources` in the ledger. Give its origin (URL), licence, author, and anything
   the licence needs, such as the credit line.
3. Add the file's row.
   - Sounds go under `audio`, keyed by sound id, with a `tier`.
   - Everything else goes under `files`, as a path glob.
4. A recorded sound replaces the synthesised one when it has the same name.
   - It must be a 16-bit WAV, mono or stereo.
   - It must sit in its tier's loudness range, measured as the loudest 100 ms in dBFS:

| Tier | Loudness range (dBFS) |
| --- | --- |
| hero | -14 to -3 |
| foreground | -24 to -8 |
| detail | -34 to -14 |
| bed | -26 to -8 |

## The mix

Buses (set up in `Game.ensure_buses`):

- Music, Ambience and SFX each have a volume setting.
- An Interior reverb send.
- A limiter on Master.

Music and Ambience duck in two ways:

- **Under gunfire and blasts:** a compressor keyed off the SFX bus.
- **Under story beats:** `Sfx.duck()`.
  - Dialogue takes music down 5 dB.
  - The aux power-up takes it down 12 dB.
  - Ambience takes half of each duck.
